The lights: a foldable rectangle per zone, with small ON and OFF buttons for the whole zone and a grid of small ON/OFF tiles, one per lamp. A lamp that does not answer is greyed out.
The slider only changes its draft while it is dragged: the value is sent on release (`onRoom` or `onLight` with `bri`), so the bridge is not flooded. ON means full power (`bri` 254); every lamp that can dim has a slider inside its block, and a small bulb at its top right whose yellow fades as the brightness drops. Rooms with at least one lamp on come first.
